# Invoice AI — Hackathon Project Brief

**Status: reusable draft. Confirm team, pilot users and evidence for the specific event before submitting.**

## Short description

Invoice AI is a finance workspace for freelancers and small businesses. It helps users create customer invoices, record payments, track supplier bills, review basic financial reports and understand what their recorded activity says about income, expenses and outstanding balances.

## Problem

Small businesses often keep sales, payments and costs across paper, messaging apps and spreadsheets. This makes it harder to see what customers still owe, what has been paid to suppliers and whether their records are complete. Accounting language and complicated organization setup can also discourage people who manage a business on their own.

## Solution

Invoice AI gives freelancers a direct path to manage invoices and business records without requiring an organization or team. Plain-language summaries explain recorded activity, while optional business and team features support users who need them. A tab-scoped offline invoice draft helps prevent work from being lost during a short interruption; the draft is clearly marked unsent and requires the user to reconnect and submit it.

## How it works

1. A freelancer or business owner creates an account and adds customer details.
2. They create invoices, record received payments, and track supplier bills and payments.
3. They review income, supplier expenses, estimated profit and outstanding balances based on saved records.
4. They may import a bank statement and match its lines to payments already recorded. Matching does not independently verify payment.
5. They can ask Invoice AI about recorded financial data. The assistant has separate tools for historical summaries, due-date cash movement and a simple next-month profit outlook based on the previous three complete months.

## Current capabilities and limits

- The profit outlook is a three-month historical average applied to the next full month. It is a rough run-rate estimate, not a statistical prediction or guarantee.
- Financial reports only include records saved in Invoice AI. Unrecorded costs such as rent, salaries or taxes may be missing.
- The cash-flow forecast is based on outstanding invoice and supplier-bill due dates. Expected payments are not guaranteed, and it is not a bank-balance forecast.
- Supplier-bill AI extraction requires internet access and a configured AI service; it is not an offline feature.
- Payment status depends on successful payment-provider verification. A screenshot, SMS or imported bank line alone is not proof of settlement.
- Invoice AI does not issue loans, make credit decisions, guarantee credit readiness or claim a credit score.

## Suggested demonstration

Use the labeled fictional demo workspace and tell judges that its records are synthetic. Show a freelancer creating an invoice, recording a partial payment, checking the balance and financial summary, then matching a sample bank-statement line to an already recorded payment. If demonstrating offline support, disconnect the browser, edit an invoice draft, reconnect and show that the user must explicitly save it. Keep payment-provider test activity separate from real payments.

## Impact measurement plan

No user-impact results are claimed in this draft. For a pilot, establish a baseline and gather consented evidence such as:

- Number of pilot users who maintain invoice and expense records for at least four weeks.
- Whether users can correctly explain an invoice balance, cash received and recorded supplier costs after using the product.
- Time and completion rate for creating an invoice and recording a payment, including a short connectivity interruption.
- Changes in completeness of invoice and supplier-bill records.
- User-reported confidence in understanding business cash flow, measured with the same questions before and after the pilot.

Report the sample size, dates, method, missing responses and limitations. Do not treat demo data, registrations or feature usage as proof of improved financial outcomes.
